Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Formula Swim iPhone app (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by Eric Doroshenko, an individual developer based in Washington, USA ("I", "me", "my"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other personal information.
  • Your scores and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device and usage data as described below.
  • Tracking with your advertising identifier only happens if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple. I never see your payment information.

Information I collect

I do not collect, store or receive any personal information through the App. There is no sign-up, no login and no server operated by me that the App sends your data to.

Your high scores, recent runs, car colour, unlocks and settings (such as units and sound volume) are saved locally on your device. They are not transmitted to me, and they are deleted if you delete the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When ads are requested and displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you tap "Allow" on Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow tracking, the IDFA is not shared.
  • IP address, and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it.
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size.
  • Usage and ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether they were viewed or tapped, and in-app activity related to ads.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash, performance and ad-loading data.

This information is used by Google for advertising (including, where you allow it, personalized ads), ad measurement and analytics, and fraud and abuse prevention. I do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; I only see aggregated reports such as the number of ads shown.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

The App offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your name, Apple ID, payment card or billing details. The App only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is active, so it can turn off ads on your device.

Your choices: opting out of tracking

  • When the App first asks, you can tap Ask App Not to Track.
  • You can change this at any time in iOS: open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off Formula Swim (or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" for all apps).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off all ads in the App, so no further ad requests are made.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored scores and settings.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, please contact me using the email below and I will take appropriate steps.

Data retention

Because I do not collect personal information, I do not retain any. Data stored on your device remains there until you delete the App. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by iOS. Communication between the App and Google's ad servers uses encrypted connections provided by Google's software.

Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features or advertising partners change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
