Formula Swim

One-finger arcade racing. Drag to steer, flick to drift.

Formula Swim is a fast, top-down open-wheel racer for iPhone. Weave through rival cars, catch a slipstream, grab gas cans and see how far you can drive.

FAQ

How do I restore Remove Ads?

If you bought Remove Ads before (for example on a new phone or after reinstalling), open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Why do I see ads?

Formula Swim is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. A short ad may play every few runs, and you can choose to watch an ad to revive once per run. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off all ads.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Scores and settings are stored only on your iPhone. There are no accounts and nothing is synced to a server, so deleting the app also deletes your scores.

How do I change units or sound?

Open Settings in the game. You can switch between miles and kilometres, turn sound on or off, and adjust the engine and effects volume separately.
